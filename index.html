      achievements

        music: built in

        Start partway through with what a strong player has by then on this difficulty (from the simulated runs). Playtest runs don't count toward stats or the leaderboard.

        • Survive one night, 10 pm to 6 am. It takes about seven minutes, and it gets worse every hour.
        • Stoke the fire (S). If it stays out, the dark comes in.
        • Eat (E) all night or you starve. Cook (C) raw meat first; raw makes you sick. Meat goes bad, raw sooner than cooked.
        • The pile by the fire only holds so much. Stack it too high and it topples into the fire.
        • Go out to gather wood (G). Any traps you've set are checked on every trip. While you're out, no one is minding the room, and something hunts the trees.
        • An amber ember glows wherever you could build something: click it. (Or press B for the full list.)
        • People knock. Not everything that knocks is a person. The door only opens if you open it.
        • Only a night survived to dawn scores: for making it, for the people you let in, and for everything you still have when the sun comes up. A challenge multiplies it.
        • Survive to dawn and you can rest through the day and face the next night, which is worse. A run is up to five nights. The third and fourth are a blizzard, where the cold can kill you and the logs come in frozen. Something else comes with the fourth. The fifth is the last.
        • On a phone, play sideways and tap the buttons.
