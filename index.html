      firelight

      keep the fire alive until dawn.

      • Survive one night, 10 pm to 6 am. It takes about seven minutes, and it gets worse every hour.
      • Stoke the fire (S). If it stays out, the dark comes in.
      • Eat (E). Run out of food and you starve.
      • Go out to gather wood (G) or hunt (H). While you're out, no one is minding the room, and something hunts the trees.
      • People knock. Not everything that knocks is a person. The door only opens if you open it.
      • Your score is what you still have at dawn. Everything you build or burn costs you.
