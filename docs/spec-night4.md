# Night 4: the guest who never knocks (spec, not built)

Written 2026-10-17 after v0.33. Night 4 is the blizzard again, plus one new monster. The door is the theme: by night 4
the player opens it constantly. The guest makes each opening a small risk without making trips a coin flip.

## The guest

**How it gets in**
- **Only when you come back from a trip.** Leaving and visitors don't count. That's one roll per trip, not per
  opening, so a 20-trip night isn't 40 chances.
- **Chance:** about 10% per return, plus 5% if you ran back with the grin up (you came in fast and didn't look).
  That's roughly 2 guests a night for a heavy-trip player and 0–1 for a careful one.
- **One at a time.** No rolls while one is already inside.
- **Salt stops it.** A salted doorway keeps it out completely, and **the line is not used up** by the guest (mimics
  still break it as now). On night 4 the salt line becomes worth its 2 salt.

**The tell: easy to miss, never invisible**
- Right after you close the door: a beat of cold draft, a low shape crossing the edge of the firelight toward a
  corner, and the line "something brushed past your legs as you came in." That line only shows when one actually
  got in.
- In its corner: two **small, dim, low** eyes, nearer the floor than the eyes in the dark, and they don't blink in
  rhythm. A soft breathing sound from that side now and then.

**Where it hides**
- In the **darkest unlit corner** (the same four corners as the torches). A torch or the woodshed candle makes a
  corner off-limits.
- **If every corner is lit, it can't settle.** It skitters along the wall and is gone the next time the door opens.
  Lighting all corners is a hard counter, at the cost of 6–8 wood plus the woodshed.

**What it does while it's in**
- **Steals:** every 20–30 seconds while the fire is at "flickering" or below, *or while you're out*, it takes one
  thing from the rack or shelf. Cured meat first, then cooked, raw, blood, then salt. Items vanish with a faint
  scrape, and the corner eyes are briefly gone.
- **Grows bolder:** after 2 in-game hours inside, it steals every 10–15 seconds regardless of the fire.
- **Lethal only through neglect:** if the fire dies while it's inside, it doesn't wait the ten seconds for the dark.
  It comes out of its corner (that's the death).

**Getting it out (pick any)**
1. **Light its corner** (a torch: 2 wood). It bolts to another dark corner, or out if there's none left.
2. **Throw salt at it** (1 salt). Click its eyes, or an ember appears over its corner when you have salt. It
   shrieks and flees out the door. This is the cheap, active answer.
3. **Leave the door open on purpose:** a new action, "hold the door open" (5 seconds). The cold pours in (the
   cold meter rises fast) and the fire drops a level, but it leaves. It's the free option that costs you warmth.

  night 4.

## Sounds and art

- **The guest:** a low, long-limbed shape about 10 px high, mostly black, seen only as it crosses the firelight;
  in its corner only the two small low eyes. Its death: it comes out of the corner along the floor, and the eyes
  are the last thing.
- **Sounds:** a soft skitter across the floor when it gets in or moves corners, breathing from its corner, a
  scrape when it steals, and a shriek when salted (reuse her shriek, pitched down).

## Achievements

- **The Guest** (death).
- **Nowhere to Hide:** it can't settle because every corner is lit.
- **Not Tonight:** the salted door keeps one out.
- **Fourth Dawn,** which exists already.

## Order to build

1. Entry, the tell and the corner logic, with the torches and candle as blockers.
2. Stealing and the bolder phase.
3. The three ways out (torch, salt, holding the door open).
4. The death scene, the sounds and the achievements.
5. Simulated players for night 4, then tuning the 10% entry chance against the balance targets.

Rough size: 1–1.5 hours.
